My Baby · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Overview

My Baby is made by Chanii Apps. We built it so your family’s information stays yours. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data, and the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What you enter

The app stores what you choose to log, such as your baby’s name, birthday, feeds, sleep, diapers, symptoms, temperatures and notes, so it can show your history, summaries and suggestions.

Where it is stored

Your entries are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they also sync through your private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit so they can appear on your other devices. Chanii Apps cannot see or access this data. You can turn off iCloud syncing for My Baby in the Settings app.

Sharing

If you choose to share a baby log, Apple’s CloudKit sharing makes that shared data available to the people you invite. Sharing is controlled by you through Apple’s services; My Baby does not send your data to Chanii Apps.

Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Feed reminders are scheduled on your device. Widgets and Live Activities read your entries on your device to show recent activity. None of this is sent to us.

Health information

Symptoms and temperatures you log are used only to display them back to you. They are not shared with Chanii Apps and are not used for advertising.

Children’s privacy

My Baby is intended for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. Information about a child is entered by their parent or caregiver and stays in that person’s control.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries at any time, or delete everything from Settings > Delete All Entries. Deleting the app removes data from the device. Data in iCloud can be removed from the Settings app under iCloud > Manage Storage.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update it here and in the app and change the effective date above. Questions about privacy? Email support.chaniiapps@gmail.com.
